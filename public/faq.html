Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about $HERO, $VETS, staking, and the HeroBase DEX aggregator on PulseChain and BASE.

What is $HERO token?
$HERO is a utility token deployed on both PulseChain and BASE networks. It powers the HeroBase DEX aggregator and supports the V.I.C. Foundation's mission to fund veterans, first responders, and their families through blockchain technology. A portion of every transaction fee is directed to the foundation's charitable programs.
What is $VETS token?
$VETS is a companion token to $HERO, also available on PulseChain. It represents the veteran community within the HeroBase ecosystem and can be traded, staked, and used within the DEX aggregator alongside $HERO.
How do I buy $HERO on PulseChain?
To buy $HERO on PulseChain: (1) Set up a Web3 wallet like MetaMask or Rabby and add the PulseChain network (Chain ID: 369). (2) Bridge PLS or USDC to PulseChain using the PulseChain bridge. (3) Visit HeroBase.io and connect your wallet. (4) Use the DEX aggregator to swap any supported token for $HERO. The aggregator automatically finds the best price across all PulseChain DEXs.
How do I buy $HERO on BASE?
To buy $HERO on BASE: (1) Set up a Web3 wallet and add the BASE network (Chain ID: 8453). (2) Bridge ETH or USDC to BASE using the official BASE bridge or Coinbase. (3) Visit HeroBase.io, switch your wallet to the BASE network, and connect. (4) Use the DEX aggregator to swap ETH or USDC for $HERO. The aggregator routes through Aerodrome, Uniswap, and other BASE DEXs for best pricing.
What is the HeroBase DEX aggregator?
HeroBase is a multi-chain DEX aggregator that finds the best swap prices across all major decentralized exchanges on PulseChain and BASE simultaneously. It supports gasless swaps, DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) orders, limit orders, portfolio tracking, and LP staking — all in one interface. It is free to use and requires only a Web3 wallet.
How do I stake $HERO?
Staking $HERO is available directly through HeroBase.io. Connect your Web3 wallet, navigate to the Staking section, select the amount of $HERO you want to stake, and confirm the transaction. Staking rewards are distributed in $HERO and a portion supports the V.I.C. Foundation's veteran programs. Unstaking is available at any time with no lock-up period.
What is the V.I.C. Foundation?
The V.I.C. Foundation (Veterans In Crypto) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that supports veterans, first responders, and their families through blockchain technology and crypto-powered charitable programs. A portion of every $HERO and $VETS transaction fee is directed to the foundation. Learn more at vicfoundation.com.
Is HeroBase safe to use?
HeroBase is a non-custodial DEX aggregator — it never holds your funds. All swaps execute directly from your wallet via audited smart contracts. The platform does not require you to create an account or provide personal information. As with all DeFi platforms, always verify you are on the correct URL (herobase.io) and never share your seed phrase with anyone.
What wallets are supported?
HeroBase supports all major Web3 wallets including MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, WalletConnect-compatible wallets, and hardware wallets like Ledger and Trezor. Any wallet that supports PulseChain (Chain ID: 369) or BASE (Chain ID: 8453) is compatible.
How can I donate to the V.I.C. Foundation using crypto?
You can donate to the V.I.C. Foundation directly using $HERO, $VETS, PLS, ETH, or USDC on PulseChain or BASE. Visit vicfoundation.com for wallet addresses and donation instructions. Every donation is transparent and trackable on-chain. The foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) and crypto donations may be tax-deductible — consult your tax advisor.
